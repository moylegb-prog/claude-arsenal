## RECORDING TIPS

1. **Resolution:** 1920x1080 minimum
2. **Clean terminal:** Hide personal info, API keys
3. **Slow mouse movements:** Easier to follow
4. **Pause between actions:** Gives time for voiceover

## POST-PRODUCTION

### Music
- Royalty-free electronic/ambient
- Suggestions:
  - Epidemic Sound (paid)
  - YouTube Audio Library (free)
  - Artlist (paid)

### Editing
- DaVinci Resolve (free)
- Or just use Windows Video Editor

### Export Settings
- 1080p, 30fps minimum
- MP4, H.264 codec
