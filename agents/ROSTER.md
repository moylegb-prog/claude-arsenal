### BYZANTINE COORDINATOR
> *"Trust no one. Verify everything."*

Implements Byzantine Fault Tolerance - the gold standard for distributed consensus. Can detect and isolate malicious or faulty nodes while maintaining system integrity.

**Key Stats:**
- Tolerates up to 33% compromised nodes
- Cryptographic verification
- Multi-round voting protocol

---

### RAFT MANAGER
> *"One leader, unified vision."*

Clean, understandable consensus through leader election. When the leader fails, a new one rises. The logs must be replicated.

**Key Stats:**
- Sub-second leader election
- Log replication guarantee
- Heartbeat monitoring

---

### GOSSIP COORDINATOR
> *"Rumors spread fast. Truth spreads faster."*

Epidemic-style information propagation. Every node tells two friends, who tell two friends. Eventually consistent, massively scalable.

**Key Stats:**
- O(log n) propagation
- Partition tolerant
- Self-healing network

---

### CRDT SYNCHRONIZER
> *"Conflicts are inevitable. Resolution is automatic."*

Conflict-free Replicated Data Types. When two nodes edit the same data, there's no conflict - the math just works.

**Key Stats:**
- Automatic merge resolution
- No coordination overhead
- Offline-capable
